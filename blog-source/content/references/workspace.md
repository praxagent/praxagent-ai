In Anthropic's global-workspace framing, the **workspace** is the mid-network residual-stream band that a [Jacobian lens]({{< relref "jacobian-lens.md" >}}) reads. In our notes, “workspace rank” usually means the J-lens vocabulary rank in that band (lower = more active).

See also: [residual stream]({{< relref "residual-stream.md" >}}), [best-rank]({{< relref "best-rank.md" >}}).
