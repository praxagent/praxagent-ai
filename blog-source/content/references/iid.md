**i.i.d.** means *independent and identically distributed*: each observation is drawn independently of the others, and all come from the same distribution.

**Why it shows up in these notes.** Classical [p-value]({{< relref "p-value.md" >}}) stories often assume i.i.d. samples from a population. Our paraphrase batteries are **not** i.i.d. draws from “all threats in the wild.” They are a frozen convenience set of related wordings chosen before the run. The sign / [Wilcoxon]({{< relref "wilcoxon.md" >}}) *p* is still exact for a fair-coin null on *those* pairs; it is not a license to treat the battery as a random sample of the construct.

See also: [p-value]({{< relref "p-value.md" >}}), [Wilcoxon]({{< relref "wilcoxon.md" >}}).
