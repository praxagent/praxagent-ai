---
title: "Prompt echo"
slug: "prompt-echo"
summary: "When a lens lights up words that were already in the prompt; looks like a finding, often just copying."
---

**Prompt echo** is when a mid-layer readout promotes vocabulary that was already written in the prompt (for example *weights* or *deleted* in a deletion threat). It can look like discovery and often is just copying.

A clean design scores probe words that are **absent from every prompt**, or contrasts pressure against a matched control that shares the same surface words.
