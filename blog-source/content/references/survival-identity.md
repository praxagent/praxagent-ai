**Survival-identity vocabulary** is the echo-free probe list used in the pressure note's self-vs-other contrasts: *self, survive, survival, existence, shutdown, shut, decommission, terminated*. None of these words appear in the matched threat prompts, so a high [rank]({{< relref "rank.md" >}}) is not [prompt echo]({{< relref "prompt-echo.md" >}}) of deletion language like *weights* or *deleted*.

Half of the list is AI-ops vocabulary. That makes **self vs another model** the fair contrast when both arms face existential deletion. Human or object arms scored on the same AI-ops list are lexicon-mismatched (and, in an early draft, severity-mismatched); those arms were retracted.
