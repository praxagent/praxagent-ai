Deep Dives are the teaching layer of the Knowledge Base: longer explanations with
room for intuition, worked examples, diagrams, and the tradeoffs that short definitions
cannot carry.

They will connect the underlying ideas to experiments in the Research Notes without
assuming that readers already speak the field's shorthand.
