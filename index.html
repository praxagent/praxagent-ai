Research & Engineering Focus

Open technical research, controlled evaluation, and systems work aimed at claims that can be inspected and reproduced.

Mechanistic Interpretability

Fitting and auditing Jacobian lenses, mapping sparse-autoencoder features, and testing what internal readouts do and do not establish.

Model Evaluation & Reproducibility

Designing controlled comparisons, pre-registered gates, null baselines, leakage checks, immutable artifacts, and public result receipts.

AI Safety & Security

Studying prompt injection, deceptive-behavior probes, guardrails, and failure modes with matched controls instead of showcase-only demos.

Agents & Human Collaboration

Building shared environments where people and AI agents work with common tools, memory, evidence, and explicit human oversight.

Retrieval & Knowledge Systems

Engineering RAG, embedding, and evidence-traceability systems that connect model outputs to inspectable source material.

Research-to-Deployment Advisory

Translating experimental evidence into evaluation plans, architecture decisions, safety controls, and realistic adoption strategy.

Why praxagent?

An independent, self-funded AI research lab and applied-systems studio working in public.

Open Artifacts

Publishing model artifacts, code, prompts, hashes, data, and receipts so readers can inspect the evidence rather than trust a summary.

Controls Before Claims

Using matched baselines, null transports, pre-registered decisions, and reported failures to keep conclusions proportional to the experiment.

Research That Has to Run

Combining mathematical analysis with the systems engineering needed to test ideas on real models, including frontier-scale open weights.
