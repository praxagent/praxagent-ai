Sampling

Choosing the next token from a distribution over the vocabulary, rather than always taking the single highest-scoring token.

Sampling means drawing the next token from a probability distribution over the vocabulary, instead of always emitting the argmax. The raw next-token scores from the unembedding are turned into probabilities (usually via softmax), then a token is chosen according to a rule such as multinomial draw, often after reshaping the distribution with temperature or truncating it with top-p.

A greedy continuation is the opposite: no sampling, just the highest-scoring token at every step. That path is deterministic given the prompt and model; sampled paths are not.

See also: temperature, top-p, greedy continuation.
